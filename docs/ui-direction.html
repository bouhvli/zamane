Luminous Glass

Zamane already has good bones and a real palette. What it doesn't have is one atmosphere. This direction takes the light, frosted language the app already reaches for in three places and makes it the whole surface — then spends its boldness in exactly one place: the numbers.

The read

Where the current build stands, honestly. This is a refinement brief, not a rebuild — most of what's here is worth keeping.

Working

  • Semantic token layer. Colour roles, z-index scale, and a reduced-motion net already exist in theme.css. Nothing needs inventing — only extending.
  • Accessibility discipline. 44px tap floor, documented contrast ratios, real headings, aria-current on the nav, focus rings everywhere.
  • Restraint. One accent, one drenched surface, one display treatment. The rules are stated in comments and mostly obeyed.
  • The frosted bottom nav. The best-resolved element in the app, and the seed of this whole direction.

Holding it back

  • Two design languages. The dark violet PageHero on Home versus the quiet light PageHeader on the other four tabs. Home and Trips don't look like the same product.
  • Glass with nothing behind it. The frosted rows only exist on Home, because only Home paints an ambient wash for them to refract. Everywhere else, backdrop-blur is blurring a flat colour — i.e. doing nothing.
  • Money isn't styled like money. The app's headline figure is set in the same face as a nav label with tabular-nums bolted on. A savings app should have a numeric voice.
  • Four hand-written shadow recipes. Card, glass row, nav, hero each invent their own — three different light sources across one screen.
  • One radius for everything. 20px on a 54px thumbnail and on a full-bleed sheet. Nested radius never derived.
  • Almost no motion. A press-scale and one width transition. Nothing about arriving, changing, or completing feels responsive.

The direction

Light-first translucency — Apple's material layering crossed with the precision of a good instrument panel. Futuristic here means crisp, lit, and exact, not dark and neon.

One light source

A violet key light from the top-right and a rose fill from the bottom-left, declared once and used by every shadow, glow and gradient in the app. Every surface is lit from the same place.

One material

Frosted white glass at four defined depths. Cards, rows, chrome and sheets differ by blur radius and shadow spread — never by a new recipe.

Numbers are the hero

Every figure in the app gets a dedicated face, tabular figures, and a subdued currency mark. The savings total is the only place gradient is allowed to touch type.

Motion means something

Four uses only: arriving (stagger), navigating (view transitions), progressing (orbit sweep), and confirming (count-up). No ambient animation.

Three rules that come with the ramps. One: a tint is a ramp step, not an alpha — bg-violet-50, never bg-primary/10, so a badge on a card and the same badge on the hero are the same colour. Two: small text on a light surface uses rose-700; rose-500 is a background colour only. Three: semantic success / warning / danger stay separate from the two brand hues — a reached goal is not "accent", it's success.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans stays and keeps every job it has. It gains one partner, with exactly one job: anything that is a number, and the four headlines that matter.

Display & data — Sora 700

Why it blends: both faces sit on a geometric skeleton with near-identical x-heights, so they read as one system at a glance, while Sora's engineered, structural forms give it clear display character against Plus Jakarta's rounder humanist ones — role separation without the mushy middle.

This started as Space Grotesk and changed during the build. Space Grotesk's numeral 1 carries a flag the full width of its tabular advance, so consecutive ones butt together: 11,100 rendered with what looked like a strike through it, and no amount of letter-spacing separated them. In an app whose headline element is a sum of money, a numeral that can be misread is disqualifying however good the rest of the face looks. Sora — named here as the alternate from the start — has unambiguous figures at every pairing.

UI & body — Plus Jakarta Sans 400 / 500 / 600 / 700

Apartment deposit

Every label, description, button, form field and paragraph in the app. Unchanged from today — it is a good face for this product and it is already loaded.

JetBrains Mono comes out. It exists today for the invite code and prices; Sora's tabular figures do both jobs better, and dropping a family removes a webfont from the critical path.

The scale. Nine steps, and figures never improvise a size: 2xs 11 · xs 12 · sm 14 · base 16 · lg 18 · xl 22 · 2xl 28 · 3xl 36 · hero 48. Display sizes tighten to -0.03em; the hero figure to -0.045em. Both faces load as one Google Fonts request, non-render-blocking.

Glass & depth

Four levels replace four hand-written recipes. Each is blur + a lit top edge + a violet-tinted cast from the same light source. Depth changes by one variable, never by improvisation.

Level 1 — rows
blur 16 · cast 12/28 · list rows, compact cards, chips
Level 2 — cards
blur 20 · cast 22/44 · content cards, empty states, tiles
Level 3 — floating chrome
blur 24 · cast 30/60 · hero panel, bottom nav, FAB
Level 4 — sheets & dialogs
blur 28 · scrim behind · bottom sheets, confirm dialogs

The ambient field is what makes this work. Today the wash lives on Home only (.dashboard-mesh). It moves to the app shell as one fixed layer behind every route — two low-opacity radial lights plus a 22px dot lattice — so glass on Trips, Shopping and Goals finally has something real to refract.

Radius gets a ramp too. xs 8 thumbs and chips · sm 12 inputs · md 16 rows · lg 20 cards · xl 28 hero and sheets · full pills. Nested radius is derived, never guessed: inner = outer − padding.

Signature moves

Six changes carry the whole redesign. Everything else in this document is the system that lets them exist without looking bolted on.

The Progress Orbit

A conic-gradient ring — violet through rose on a hairline track — replacing the linear bar wherever the bar is competing for horizontal space. One component, three sizes, no SVG and no library. It sweeps from zero to its value on mount, which turns "how are we doing" into a moment rather than a readout.

The five others

  • Ambient field, app-wide. One fixed layer in AppLayout; delete the Home-only version. Every tab gets the same atmosphere, and every glass surface starts working.
  • Light instrument panel. The dark PageHero becomes Level-3 glass. The figure keeps its crown-moment weight through a violet→rose gradient fill and a hairline arc instead of a drenched slab. This is the change that makes Home and Trips the same product.
  • Bento home. Hero, then a two-up row of instrument tiles (shopping count, next trip countdown), then the goal feed. Scale contrast is the difference between a designed dashboard and a stack of equal cards.
  • View transitions. <Link viewTransition> plus a view-transition-name on the thumbnail and title, so a card's photo morphs into the detail hero. React Router 7 supports it natively — roughly fifteen lines for the strongest "this feels expensive" cue available on mobile.
  • A real skeleton system. A <Skeleton> primitive on Level-1 glass with a shimmer travelling along the ambient light direction, used on every route. The boot screen becomes the Home skeleton rather than a spinner and a joke.

Motion budget. 120ms press · 200ms colour and state · 320ms enter and exit · 520ms orbit sweep and count-up. Easing cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1) throughout, one spring reserved for the FAB sheet. Transform and opacity only. All of it already covered by the existing prefers-reduced-motion net.

Ship gate

Nothing merges until all of it passes, on a real phone.

  • Body text ≥ 4.5:1 and UI boundaries ≥ 3:1 — on glass, over the ambient field, in both themes
  • Every interactive target ≥ 44×44px, focus ring visible on all of them
  • prefers-reduced-motion kills the orbit sweep, the count-up, the stagger and the view transitions
  • No backdrop-filter without an opaque fallback background behind it
  • Blur layer count ≤ 3 in any one viewport — measured on a mid-range Android, not a desktop
  • Empty and loading states designed for every route before the populated state ships
  • No colour outside the ramps; no shadow outside the four glass levels; no radius off the scale
  • The savings figure still reads correctly at MAD 999,999 and at 0
